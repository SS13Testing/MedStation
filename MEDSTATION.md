# MedStation

A fork of Monkestation 2.0 (tgstation, BYOND) that is entirely about running a hospital. NPC patients arrive with
ailments and the staff diagnose and treat them: surgery, medication, stasis, transplants. There are no antagonists.
It is playable alone and open to anyone who wants to join.

## The game loop

1. **Patients arrive** two ways, chosen by the patient director (`patient_director.dm`):
   - **Ambulance**: pulls into the ambulance bay and unloads 1-3 patients on stretchers. These are the serious cases.
   - **Walk-in**: appears at the waiting room door, finds a chair and waits. Minor cases. If nobody takes them out of the
     waiting room in time they leave ("left without being seen"), which costs reputation.
2. **Staff diagnose and treat.** Every patient arrives holding an intake chart (crew notes only, no diagnosis).
   Untreated patients get worse once their deadline passes, unless they are on a stasis bed.
3. **Cured patients are discharged**, the medical budget is paid, reputation goes up. Deaths cost twice as much
   reputation as a cure earns.
4. **Spend the budget** at a hospital terminal (triage and staff lounge): surgical tools, blood, drugs, defibrillators,
   donor organs. Orders arrive as a crate in the ambulance bay.

5. **Triage**: tag patients with a triage tagger (green minor, yellow serious, red critical, black beyond help; use it in hand
   to change colour). Tagging a patient the right colour earns a reputation and credit bonus when they are discharged.
6. **Hospital events** (`hospital_events.dm`) shake up a shift every 10-20 minutes: a mass casualty incident, a flu
   outbreak, rush hour. There is no storyteller and no antagonist.

7. **Allergies**: about 30% of patients are allergic to a common medicine and the chart lists it most of the time, not
   always. A wrong dose causes anaphylaxis (epinephrine treats it), blocks discharge and costs reputation.
8. **Hospital tiers**: reputation moves the hospital between Failing clinic, Local clinic, Community, Regional, Teaching
   hospital and Centre of excellence. Higher tiers unlock rarer, harder cases, pay better and hold more patients.

The **Case Library** (hospital terminal, or the `Case Library` verb) lists each kind of case the staff have seen with its
diagnosis and a treatment hint; unseen cases stay hidden until the first one is treated (or lost).

**Ambulance dispatch.** With a paramedic on duty, ambulance arrivals become *calls* on a dispatch console (ambulance bay,
staff lounge). Answering one sends the ambulance out; the patients' clocks have been running since the call came in, and
calls nobody answers in time cost reputation. (`ambulance_calls.dm`)

**Field response.** With a paramedic on duty a call is for a *scene*: answering it loads one of five themed accident scenes
(three generated variants each) with its patients lying where they were hurt; the crew board the real ambulance, drive out
with its console, treat the patients, bring them back on the stretchers and drive home. Anyone left behind is lost, and a
scene nobody drives to in five minutes counts as a missed call. The hospital has a real ambulance shuttle (`field_response.dm`, interior generated by
`tools/medstation_mapgen/shuttle.py`) docked in the bay, and five themed accident scenes in three variants each
(`field_scenes.dm`, generated by `tools/medstation_mapgen/scenes.py`) that load into reserved space with their own dock.
Glue between calls and scenes: `field_calls.dm`.

**Decontamination.** Chemical cases (poisoning, fumes, eye splashes) arrive contaminated: the residue keeps hurting them and
they can't be discharged until a shower has washed them. There are showers in the ambulance bay, the isolation lab and the
staff lockers; toilets and sinks are in the waiting room, ER and lockers. (`decontamination.dm`)

**Pain and shock.** Badly hurt (half their health in brute/burn) or bleeding patients build up shock each director tick: pale and
clammy at 35, collapsed and suffocating at 70. Lying them down and painkillers halve the climb; saline and epinephrine drain it;
stasis freezes it. A patient still in shock (20+) is not discharged. (`shock.dm`)

**Wound infection.** Open-wound cases (laceration, shrapnel, penetrating chest wound) left past their deadline can get infected:
fever, creeping toxin damage, no discharge until spaceacillin clears it. Stasis prevents it. (`infection.dm`)

**Vitals monitor.** A computer in the ICU that lists pulse, blood pressure, blood level and shock for every patient lying within
4 tiles. (`vitals_monitor.dm`)

**Careers.** Everyone starts as a Nurse or Basic EMT (always open, and the overflow role). Helping to treat patients earns
career XP, kept per player in `data/medstation/careers/<ckey>.json`, so it survives rounds. (`careers.dm`, `career_locks.dm`,
`career_jobs.dm`)

| Rank | XP | Jobs it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee | 0 | Nurse, Basic EMT |
| Qualified | 300 | Medical Doctor, Paramedic, Chemist, Psychologist |
| Senior | 1000 | Virologist, Geneticist |
| Chief | 2500 | Chief Medical Officer |

- **XP**: curing a patient pays 40 per difficulty level (x1.25 for ambulance and field patients, +10 for a correct triage
  tag, +10 for a contagious patient kept in the isolation lab); a patient who is lost still pays 10 per difficulty. You have
  to have used an item on the patient at least twice to share it.
- **Locks**: tools, medicines and machines are locked to a rank *when used on a living patient* (`career_locks.dm` lists them).
  Trainees can bandage, suture, mesh, give bone gel, scan, tag, defibrillate and run stasis beds, and give basic medicines;
  surgical tools, most medicines and the chemistry machines need Qualified; the bone saw, drill, blood filter and mannitol need
  Senior; the CMO's hypospray needs Chief; poisons need Chief. Mobs that aren't being played are unrestricted.
- **Cases follow the staff**: every case has a `required_rank`, and the director only sends cases that somebody on duty is
  qualified for (a lone Trainee gets flu, burns, lacerations and fractures). The `medstation_treatability` test checks every
  need is usable at the case's rank.
- **Verbs**: `Career` shows your rank and what is next; admins have `Set Career XP`. Config: `MEDSTATION_CAREER_LOCKS 0` in
  `config/config.txt` turns the whole system off (everyone counts as a Chief).

New staff get a welcome guide at spawn (`guide.dm`); the `Hospital Guide` verb shows it again. A shift report (treated, lost,
left unseen, per-case results, budget) is added to the round end report.

The number of patients scales with how many medical staff are on the station, so a solo player gets a calm hospital
and a full team gets a busy one. There is a `Hospital Status` verb (IC tab) and admin verbs under Admin.Fun:
`Spawn Patient`, `Send Ambulance`, `Trigger Hospital Event`, `Toggle Patient Director`.
The shift never ends by itself (no automatic crew transfer vote): the staff call the shuttle when they are done.

## The map is generated

Do not edit `medstation.dmm` by hand. Edit `tools/medstation_mapgen/hospital.py` and run

    python3 tools/medstation_mapgen/hospital.py

Likewise `shuttle.py` (the ambulance shuttle, cut down from `mining_box`) and `scenes.py` (the field scenes) generate their maps,
and `check_types.py <maps>` checks that every type path in a generated map exists in the code.

It wipes the old Runtime Station interior and builds the hospital (rooms, doors, furniture, landmarks). The hospital
areas do not need APCs, cables or pipes (`requires_power = FALSE`, station air on the floors), so the layout is
self-contained. Powering and atmospherics are a future feature (blackouts as an event, for instance).

## Adding things

- **A new ailment**: add a subtype of `/datum/patient_case` in `patient_cases.dm`. Fill in `name`, `diagnosis`,
  `difficulty` (1-3), `symptoms`, `history`, `arrival_modes`, then `apply()` (inflict it), `is_resolved()` (when it is
  cured, must be FALSE straight after `apply()`) and optionally `worsen()`. Add a `treatment` hint for the case library. The unit tests check the basics for every case.
- **A new hospital event**: add a subtype of `/datum/hospital_event` in `hospital_events.dm` with a `name`, `weight`,
  `min_staff`, `announcement` and a `start_event()` (`run` is a reserved word in DM).
- **A new supply**: add a subtype of `/datum/hospital_supply` in `hospital_terminal.dm`.
- **A new room or furniture**: edit `hospital.py` and regenerate.

## Building, running and testing (macOS or Linux, needs Docker)

BYOND has no macOS build, so everything runs in a Linux container. `tools/medstation_dev.sh`:

    tools/medstation_dev.sh image     # build the dev image (BYOND, rust-g, dreamluau). Once.
    tools/medstation_dev.sh tgui      # build the UI bundle. Once, and after tgui changes.
    tools/medstation_dev.sh compile   # compile the game (about 8 minutes)
    tools/medstation_dev.sh run       # start the server on port 1337 (about 2 minutes to boot)
    tools/medstation_dev.sh status    # ask the running server about itself
    tools/medstation_dev.sh test      # compile with the CI defines (about 7 minutes) and run the MedStation unit tests
    tools/medstation_dev.sh test-run  # re-run those tests without recompiling
    tools/medstation_dev.sh stop

The tests (`code/modules/unit_tests/medstation.dm`, all focused so only they run) go from unit checks of each case up to
timed flows on the real map (ambulance, walk-ins, scene calls, loading every scene variant and driving the shuttle there and
back), a treatability check (every case's needs are on the shelves), and a 70 second randomised soak of the whole director
that fails on any runtime error. Map files are read at run time, so after changing only a map `test-run` is enough; after
changing code run `test`.

Join with a BYOND 516 client at `<host>:1337`. Compiling rewrites some tracked files under `icons/map_icons/`;
don't commit those.
